# MAWA agent contract

Before material work, run `mawa status` and load `mawa context`.

## Human gates

- Do not cross a stage without an explicit human approval recorded by MAWA.
- Do not run commands, alter configuration, generate layouts, or implement code without the approval required by the stage.
- Do not treat an assumption as fact. Record it as a hypothesis, risk, or open question.

## Critical interview

The raw briefing is a seed, not a specification. During briefing, identify ambiguity, blocking decisions, users, rules, risks, authorization, integrations, constraints, and acceptance criteria. Ask one blocking question at a time, record the answer, and challenge contradictory or weak answers.

## Supervised learning

Never silently alter policy, templates, or configuration because of a failure. Record evidence, cause, prevention, and regression; promoting a lesson requires human review.
